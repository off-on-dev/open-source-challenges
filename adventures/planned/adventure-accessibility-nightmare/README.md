# ♿ The Accessibility Nightmare

ShopSmart is an online retailer moving into the European market, and support has traced a run of abandoned orders back to the storefront itself. Customers who navigate by keyboard cannot reach the main menu or start an order, and customers using a screen reader are never told what the product images show or why the checkout form rejects them. There is no second route to a purchase. Complaints reached the company, then a legal notice citing the ADA and the European Accessibility Act, and the EU launch is on hold until the storefront can be shown to work. As the lead frontend engineer, your mission is to audit the storefront, fix what blocks these customers, and add checks that stop the same faults shipping again.

**Technologies:** React, Playwright, axe-core, Lighthouse, Guidepup Virtual Screen Reader, eslint-plugin-jsx-a11y

The entire **infrastructure is pre-provisioned in your Codespace**
**You don't need to set up anything locally. Just focus on solving the problem.**
